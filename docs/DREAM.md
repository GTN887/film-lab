# Dream / Lucid Layer

Pause a **sleeping** take on Take Board. Click the person → **Dream about…**. Film Lab opens a linked lucid sequence under that sleeper: child dream layers, then wake. Animate the dream takes. Stitch **sleep → dream → wake**.

No extra hub card. Shot form stays 25 fields. Writing stays five modes — the dream sheet is **director rewrite**.

Movie / Jedi / lucid-dream references are **inspired only**. Cast is original Character Bible. Never franchise likenesses or plots. Zero credits.

## Flow

1. Animate a **sleeping** parent take (Alison, Bradley, or any bible face).
2. Take Board — **pause** the player. Pause does not edit.
3. **Click the person** on the cast strip. The **Dream about…** popup opens.
4. Pick **Dream style** on the popup (and the Take Board accordion):
   - **Enter dream** — leave the sleeping shot; they are **inside** the sequence.
   - **Thought bubble** — click the head / person; overlay stays **on the sleeping shot** (still, mini-clip, or styled text).
5. Pick **Inner vision** (dream / daydream / thinking / lucid) and **Text stylize** (comic bubble / soft subtitle / diary caption / none).
6. Pick **Actor A / B / C** (Character Bible multi-select) and type the dream (free text).
7. Add beats **layer by layer** (title + prompt → **Add this beat**), or let Open sequence split the free text.
8. Drop **image + video look-refs** to guide the dream look (copied into `dream_refs/`). A video look-ref can fill the bubble as a mini-clip (first frame on the plate; click plays the clip).
9. **Open dream sequence** — SLEEP → DREAM layers → WAKE appear under that sleeper.
10. Click a DREAM card that is still a plate → Motion Desk **Animate**. Click a ready clip to play.
11. **Stitch sleep → dream → wake**.

Mark the **head** in Direct still opens a dream (same layer). Writing Studio can still fill a director-rewrite beat sheet.

Dream style on the popup:

| Style | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Enter dream** | Transition out of the bedroom. They are inside the dream sequence. Stitch sleep → dream → wake. |
| **Thought bubble** | Overlay on the sleeping shot. Content can be a look-ref still, a mini-clip, or styled text. |

Inner vision (prompt language — inspired only, original characters):

| Vision | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **dream** | Full dream. They are inside. |
| **daydream** | Waking daydream. Eyes soft. The room can stay. |
| **thinking** | Inner thought, not a cutaway world. |
| **lucid** | They know they are dreaming. |

Prompt text stylize (thought-bubble plate):

| Text | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **comic bubble** | Gold oval + tail + caption. |
| **soft subtitle** | Soft bar under the sleeping frame. |
| **diary caption** | Cream scrap caption. Not a franchise font. |
| **none** | Still or mini-clip only. No text. |

Old `cut` layers load as **Enter dream**. Old `lucid overlay` layers load as **Thought bubble** + **lucid**.

## Safety

- Regular / story for Teen / Child / Infant (a story crush is not sex).
- Intimate / explicit dream beats: **adult 18+ ONLY**. The layer does not unlock minors.
- Undress / explicit wardrobe is unchanged — still 18+ Explicit + adult cast.
