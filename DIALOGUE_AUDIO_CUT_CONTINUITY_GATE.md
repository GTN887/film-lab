# Dialogue & Audio Cut Continuity Gate

Film Lab now analyzes decoded PCM at consecutive Selected-Take boundaries for RMS/peak level and near-silence changes, carries persisted dialogue timing evidence, and honors explicit Director J-cut/L-cut plus intentional level/silence-change metadata.

Truth boundary: room tone is a level/silence proxy only. Film Lab does not infer semantic dialogue, speaker identity, acoustics, microphone perspective, or artistic correctness from PCM amplitude. J/L cuts are explicit Director intent, not guessed.
