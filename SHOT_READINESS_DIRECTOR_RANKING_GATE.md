# Shot Readiness & Director Take Ranking Gate

Film Lab may rank Takes using persisted machine QC evidence, but it must never convert that ranking into automatic Director selection.

## REAL
- Per-Take deterministic QC score from persisted continuity events.
- Missing media and missing scan evidence are explicitly penalized/reported.
- Shot readiness reports PASS / REVIEW / FAIL / NOT TESTED.
- Best machine-evidence candidate can be recommended for Director review.
- Existing Selected Take remains unchanged.
- Report persists in the project.

## Truth boundary
A high QC score is not a percentage measure of artistic quality and does not prove Character identity, anatomy, acting, story quality, or Creator preference. Real generated-footage acceptance remains a Creator decision.
